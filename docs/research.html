Our Research & Development

At AMBOSS, we are committed to pushing the boundaries of 3D printing technology. Our dedicated research team works tirelessly to innovate and improve our processes, materials, and applications.

Material Science

We are constantly experimenting with new filament formulations to create stronger, more durable, and more sustainable materials for a wide range of industrial and consumer applications.

Advanced Manufacturing

Our research focuses on optimizing printing parameters and developing new techniques to achieve higher resolution, faster production speeds, and superior part quality.

Sustainability

We are exploring eco-friendly materials and waste reduction methods to minimize our environmental footprint and promote a greener future for manufacturing.
