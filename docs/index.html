Materials

PLA+

An enhanced version of standard PLA. It's easy to print, biodegradable, and offers improved strength and durability. Ideal for prototypes, decorative items, and models.

ABS

A tough, impact-resistant material used to make LEGO® bricks. It's great for durable parts that need to withstand wear and tear. Requires a heated bed to prevent warping

TPU

A flexible, rubber-like filament. Known for its high elasticity and abrasion resistance, it's perfect for phone cases, gaskets, and other parts that need to bend and absorb shock.

PETG

Combines the best of PLA and ABS. It's durable, water-resistant, and easier to print than ABS. A great all-around choice for functional parts and mechanical components.

PA12-CF

A high-performance blend of Nylon 12 and carbon fiber. Exceptionally strong, lightweight, and dimensionally stable. Ideal for end-use parts, jigs, and fixtures requiring a high strength-to-weight ratio.

PC-CF

A composite of Polycarbonate and carbon fiber, offering outstanding heat resistance and strength. This is one of the strongest filaments available, perfect for demanding industrial and automotive applications

Nylon

A strong, versatile material known for its high durability, flexibility, and resistance to abrasion. It's a great choice for functional parts, gears, and living hinges.
